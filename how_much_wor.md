# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: June 2026**

## Overview

HowMuchWor is a simple bill-splitting calculator. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information.

## Data Collection

**HowMuchWor does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.** All calculations are performed locally on your device. No information you enter (currency, amounts, number of people) is saved, logged, or sent to any server.

## Internet Access

The app requests internet permission solely for loading React Native development bundles during debugging. The released app does not make any network requests.

## Third-Party Services

HowMuchWor does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## Data Storage

The app does not write any files, use persistent storage, or maintain any database. All entered data exists only in memory while the app is running and is discarded when the app is closed.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page.

## Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue at the project repository.
