# Privacy Policy for MoodTrip

**Last updated:** October 11, 2025

MoodTrip respects your privacy.  
This app does **not collect, store, or share any personal data** from users.

## Information Collection and Use
MoodTrip does not require you to create an account or provide any personal information.  
All your data (such as moods, notes, or preferences) is stored **locally on your device** and is **never transmitted** to any external server.

## Third-Party Services
MoodTrip does not use third-party analytics, ads, or tracking tools.

## Data Security
Since all data is stored on your local device, you are fully in control of your information.  
If you uninstall the app, all locally stored data will be permanently deleted.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.  
Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.
